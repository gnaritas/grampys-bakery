A bakery that can turn out baguettes and pumpernickel alongside pecan pies is a bakery with a real production line — Grampy’s is built that way from day one.

Alongside the bakes

Gourmet & specialty coffees

Coffee was on the family’s announcement right next to the menu: “Gourmet and specialty coffees also will be available” — the announced companion to a morning Danish, a soft roll, or a slice of coconut cake.

The bakery sits on Griffin Road just off U.S. 441 — an easy stop on the everyday drive, not a destination you plan around a schedule.

The buildout

Built by a builder

Three industrial-size mixers, a second convection oven and two massive refrigerators — the production kit behind the bread list. Founder Shawnn Lynch is a kitchen-and-bath contractor and built the kitchen out himself.
