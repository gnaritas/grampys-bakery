Why the cases stay full

A kitchen built for the job

Grampy’s is not a hobby oven. The buildout grew into a serious production bakery: a second convection oven, two massive refrigerators, and three industrial-size mixers, per the coverage of the build. Founder Shawnn Lynch is a kitchen-and-bath contractor by trade — he took on the building’s repairs and upgrades himself — so the kitchen was built by someone who builds for a living.

What that means for a holiday order: capacity. The kitchen was planned to keep the display cases full through the holiday season.
