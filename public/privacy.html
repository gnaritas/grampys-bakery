Plain language, on purpose

Privacy notice

This site collects as little as it possibly can. Here is the whole list.

What the contact form collects

When you use the contact form, you give us your name, your email, optionally a phone number, and your message. That information is delivered to Grampy’s Bakery so the team can answer you — orders, questions, and requests are what it’s for.

Who reads it

The bakery team. Messages go to the shop’s inbox (grampysbakery@yahoo.com) and are used only to reply to you and to handle your order. We don’t sell it, rent it, or add you to mailing lists you didn’t ask for.

Cookies, trackers, ads

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party JavaScript. No advertising networks, no analytics scripts, no social widgets. The map preview only loads when you look at it, and Google’s own privacy policy applies to that preview.

Getting your message deleted

Email grampysbakery@yahoo.com and ask. Any message you’ve sent through this form can be deleted on request — no explanation needed.

This page describes this website only. In-person purchases at the bakery follow normal cash-register rules.
