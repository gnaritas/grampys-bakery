Questions

Asked and answered

When does Grampy’s Bakery open?

The grand opening is Tuesday, November 24, 2026 at 11:45 a.m., when the Leesburg Area Chamber of Commerce cuts the ribbon at 1410 Griffin Road. Opening-week hours are announced before we open.

Where is Grampy’s Bakery located?

At 1410 Griffin Road in Leesburg, Florida — just off U.S. 441.

What’s on the menu?

Announced for the holiday season: scratch pies (blueberry, cherry, lemon, meringue, banana cream, Boston cream, pecan, coconut custard), specialty cakes (carrot, black forest, triple chocolate, German chocolate, coconut, cheesecakes), cookies, cannoli, black-and-whites, eclairs, cream puffs, crumb cakes, muffins, cupcakes, Napoleons, fruit and cheese Danish, croissants, quick nut breads, and a full bread program — plus gourmet and specialty coffees.

Will you serve coffee?

Yes — gourmet and specialty coffees alongside the bakes.

Who runs Grampy’s Bakery?

It’s a family affair. Founder Shawnn Lynch runs the bakery with family members Lorie Howell and Heather Lynch. “Grampy” is what Shawnn’s grandchildren call him, and an homage to the grandfather who taught him his work ethic.
